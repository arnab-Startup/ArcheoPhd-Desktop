The foregoing references to Elephanta are by no means exhaustive—for instance, in 1695 the Italian traveller John Francis Ganelli Careri visited Elephanta—but these references bring out the general curiosity about this easily accessible monument and also reveal the general line of speculation. On Kanheri Careri's description is the most detailed and thus most important. He devoted a whole chapter of his work to the description of Kanheri. The pagod or temple of the Canarin, whereof I intended to give an exact and true account, is one of the greatest wonders in Asia; as well because it is looked upon as the work of Alexander the Great, as for its extraordinary and incomparable workmanship, which certainly could be undertaken by none but Alexander" This interpretation notwithstandinc Careri's description of Kanheri is objective and generally accurate.
