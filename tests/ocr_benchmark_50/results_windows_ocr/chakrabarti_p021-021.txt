The Beginning ofa Systematic Scholarly Interest The turning point was the middle of the eighteenth century when there were two important lines of development. First, accurate and precise descriptions of monuments began to appear, and secondly, research began primarily on the historical geography of the country. Carsten Niebuhr, a captain of engineers in the service of the king of Denmark, came to Bombay from Mokha in Arabia on 13 September 1763. The second volume of his Voyage en Arabie and en d'autres Pays Circonvoisins published in Amsterdaxn in 1780 contains nine illustrations of Elephanta.54 An abridged English translation of his work was published in Edinburgh in 1792. Niebuhr was possibly the first to concentrate almost exclusively on Elephanta. The section 30, chapter 6 of the second volume of his work contains his observa- tions on this site.
