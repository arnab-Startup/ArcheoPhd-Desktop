In the centre of this Island there exists that wonderful pagoda of Canari, thus called fiom its being supposed to have been the work of the Canaras. It is constructed at the foot ofa great Hill of stone of light grey colour; there is a beautiful hall at its entrance, and in the yard that leads to the front back door, there are two human figures engraved on the same stone, twice as big as the giants exhibited on the procession of the Corpus Christi Feast in Lisbon, so beautiful, elegant, and so well executed, that even in silver they could not be better wrought and made with such perfection .. ..
