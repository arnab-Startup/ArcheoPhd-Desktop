weather. The analysis of seeds, molluscs, insects and bones of the
smaller mammals, birds and fishes can produce quantity of
material from the excavation. The extraction of pollen would
supplement the information. The wet-sieving and flotation
methods served as useful technique in this area of research. As in
all other methods, the larger and more representative samples
would provide valid results. E‘he evidence acquired from

order to assess the essential variables that might have influenced
the operation of cultural systems.

e. Settlement Archaeology
CScttlement archacology is the study of the spatial distribution of

an ancient human activities and occupation, ranging from the
differential location of activities within a single room to the
arrangement of sites in a region. They are concerned with
locational information. Settlement studies use features and sites as
their principle databases. The spatial patterning of archacological
features is analysed in order to reconstruct past decisions
regarding use of environment, allocation of natural resources,

f. Cognitive Archaeology

¢ (fogﬁtive archaeology is one of the newer branches of modern
