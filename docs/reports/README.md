## Archaeological Epistemology Principles

1. **Topological Superposition**: An excavation matrix is not an attribute spreadsheet; it is a directed acyclic graph (DAG) of physical events governed by the Law of Superposition and Law of Original Horizontality.
2. **Asymmetrical Chronology**: Archaeological dates are bounded by Terminus Post Quem (TPQ) and Terminus Ante Quem (TAQ) anchors and Bayesian radiocarbon calibration density envelopes, never flat uniform Gantt rectangles.
3. **Contextual Provenance**: An evidence graph must incorporate taphonomic weighting ($w_i$), differentiating primary in-situ sealed stratum finds ($w = 0.95$) from secondary unstratified surface finds ($w = 0.20$).
4. **Integrated Workbench**: Visualization instruments must be directly embedded and co-synchronized within the workstation, preventing context loss and navigation friction.
